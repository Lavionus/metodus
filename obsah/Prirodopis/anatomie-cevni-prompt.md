Full body FROM THE FRONT in anatomical standing pose, palms facing forward, arms slightly spread, entire head and feet visible, body centered around x512 with generous empty margins for interactive labels. Neutral translucent pale beige body silhouette with no skin detail, no face detail, no genitals. Heart in the middle of the chest slightly left of the midline (viewer right), realistic red-and-blue heart. Arteries painted bright red, veins painted blue, clearly separated and running side by side in limbs. Clearly show: aortic arch above the heart, descending thoracic and abdominal aorta along the spine, common carotid arteries in the neck next to blue internal jugular veins, subclavian and brachial arteries and veins down the arms branching into radial and ulnar vessels to the hands, superior vena cava and inferior vena cava entering the heart, pulmonary trunk and pulmonary vessels to faint ghosted pale lungs, renal arteries and veins to faint ghosted kidneys, iliac arteries and veins in the pelvis, femoral arteries and veins down the thighs, tibial vessels down the calves to the feet, fine branching small vessels in head, hands and feet. Anatomically plausible, no invented organs, no nerves, no extra inset panels. Clean uncluttered precise teaching image; enlarged vessel thickness for visibility.
