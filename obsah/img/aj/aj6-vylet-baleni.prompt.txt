Use case: illustration-story. First of six consistent wide story cards for a Czech grade-6 English past-tense lesson. The recurring protagonist is a 12-year-old girl with one long brown braid, a red zip jacket, yellow T-shirt, blue jeans and a teal backpack. In her bedroom in the morning she packs a striped swimsuit, towel, book and sun hat into an open small suitcase while checking a simple blank paper list. Cheerful anticipation, clear action, no other person. Refined watercolor and gouache schoolbook illustration with delicate ink contours on warm ivory paper, friendly natural proportions, wide 3:2 composition. No readable text, letters, numbers, logos, brands, watermark, speech bubbles, border, or arrows.
