Use case: historical-scene. Scientific paleoart reconstruction for a Czech grade-9 geology lesson. Wide Carboniferous coal-swamp forest: towering scale trees (Lepidodendron and Sigillaria) with diamond-patterned trunks, jointed Calamites horsetails, large tree ferns, dense clubmoss vegetation, shallow dark swamp water and humid mist. A large Meganeura-like dragonfly flies in the foreground and a small early amphibian rests near the water. No grass, flowers, modern broadleaf trees, birds, mammals, dinosaurs, or humans. Refined watercolor and gouache natural-history reconstruction with delicate ink contours, humid green and amber palette, wide 3:2 composition. No text, letters, numbers, labels, arrows, border, logo, or watermark.
