Use case: historical-scene. Scientific paleoart reconstruction for a Czech grade-9 geology lesson. Wide underwater scene of a shallow Paleozoic sea, approximately Ordovician: several accurately shaped trilobites crawling on a rippled seabed, stalked crinoids filtering in the current, brachiopod shells, a straight-shelled orthoconic nautiloid swimming above, and small early jawless fish in the distance. Clear blue-green water with sunbeams, carbonate sand and rocks; organisms separated clearly enough for classroom observation. No modern coral reef, flowering plants, modern fish, marine reptiles, dinosaurs, or humans. Refined watercolor and gouache natural-history reconstruction with delicate ink contours, warm ivory undertone, wide 3:2 composition. No text, letters, numbers, labels, arrows, border, logo, or watermark.
